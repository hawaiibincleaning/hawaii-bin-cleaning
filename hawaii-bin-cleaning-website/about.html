Why we started

Collection day takes the trash—not what it leaves behind.

In Hawaiʻi’s warm climate, residue inside household bins can quickly turn into stubborn smells, grime and an invitation for unwanted pests.

Most homeowners do not have the time, equipment or desire to scrub large carts by hand—and a quick spray can send dirty water across the driveway or toward the street. Hawaii Bin Cleaning is creating a route-based alternative that fits around normal collection day.

The centerpiece is a fully equipped Ford E-450 bin-cleaning truck with specialized cleaning heads, a dual hydraulic cart lifter, 550 gallons of fresh water and a 525-gallon tank for recovered wastewater. It is built to clean bins as a system, not improvise with a pressure-washing gun.

We are beginning with community interest so the first routes can be planned efficiently around the Oʻahu neighborhoods asking for service.
