Usually, no. Your cart needs to be empty, accessible and placed in the location provided in your service instructions. Final access and notification details will be confirmed when scheduling opens.

🚚 Cleaning process
How does professional trash bin cleaning work?

After your cart is emptied, leave it accessible at the curb. Our truck positions compatible bins over a contained cleaning area, washes and deodorizes them, and recovers the used wash water onboard. See the full curbside cleaning process.

How long does cleaning take?

A purpose-built system is designed for efficient route service, but actual time depends on cart quantity, condition, buildup and access. We will provide realistic service windows when scheduling opens.

Which types of bins can be cleaned?

We plan to service common residential trash, recycling and green-waste carts when their size, design, condition and contents are compatible with our equipment. Damaged, overloaded or unusually configured carts may not be serviceable.

What if my bin was not emptied?

A full or partially full cart generally cannot be cleaned properly. We will provide missed-collection and rescheduling instructions before paid service begins.

🚛 Truck & wastewater
How is your truck different from a pressure washer?

Our Ford E-450 is an integrated bin-cleaning platform. It carries fresh water, uses hydraulic cart lifters and specialized cleaning heads, directs wash water into a contained hopper and stores recovered gray water separately. Explore the truck and equipment.

Do you need to connect to my water supply?

The truck is equipped with a 550-gallon fresh-water tank, so the planned curbside process brings its own cleaning-water supply.

What happens to the dirty wastewater?

The truck is designed to recover wastewater from the cleaning process rather than leave it running across the driveway or toward a storm drain. Final disposal will follow the authorizations and requirements applicable to our operation.

Why hire a professional instead of using a hose?

A DIY wash still leaves you to lift or reach into the cart, handle stuck-on residue and decide where the dirty runoff goes. Our equipment is designed to do the lifting, interior washing and wastewater recovery as one curbside process.

✨ Results & benefits
Can bin cleaning help with odors?

Cleaning removes residue behind many common bin odors, so most carts should smell noticeably fresher. Results can vary based on age, damage, absorbed material, previous use and what enters the cart after service.

Can cleaning deter bugs, pests or rodents?

Removing food residue and buildup can make a bin less attractive to flies, roaches and other pests. Bin cleaning is a helpful sanitation step, but it is not pest-control service and cannot guarantee that pests or rodents will stay away.

How often should residential bins be cleaned?

Frequency depends on household use, weather, cart contents and your tolerance for buildup. Some homes may prefer recurring care, while others may need a seasonal or one-time reset. Final plan options will be explained before launch.

Does professional cleaning remove every germ?

No responsible service should promise complete germ removal without a validated product and process. Our published claims will match the actual cleaning and deodorizing products used when service launches.
