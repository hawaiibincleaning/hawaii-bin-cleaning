🚚 Cleaning process
How does professional trash bin cleaning work?

Leave empty carts curbside after collection. Our dual hydraulic lifter positions each cart over specialized cleaning heads, the interior is washed and deodorized, and the dirty water is recovered onboard. See the full curbside cleaning process.

How long does cleaning take?

A purpose-built system is designed for efficient route service, but actual time depends on cart quantity, condition, buildup and access. We will provide realistic service windows when scheduling opens.

Which types of bins can be cleaned?

We plan to service standard residential trash, recycling and green-waste carts when their size, condition and design are compatible with our equipment.

What if my bin was not emptied?

A full or blocked bin cannot be cleaned properly and may need to be skipped or rescheduled according to the future route policy.

🚛 Truck & wastewater
How is your truck different from a pressure washer?

Our Ford E-450 is a complete residential bin-cleaning system. It carries fresh water, lifts carts hydraulically, uses specialized Bolondi cleaning heads and stores recovered wastewater in a separate gray-water tank. Read more on the purpose-built truck page.

Do you need to connect to my water supply?

The truck is equipped with a 550-gallon fresh-water tank, so the planned curbside process brings its own cleaning-water supply.

What happens to the dirty wastewater?

Wash water falls into the truck's contained hopper and is stored in a 525-gallon gray-water tank rather than being left across the driveway or sent toward the storm drain.

Why hire a professional instead of using a hose?

A DIY wash still leaves you to lift or reach into the cart, handle stuck-on residue and decide where the dirty runoff goes. Our equipment is designed to do the lifting, interior washing and wastewater recovery as one curbside process.

✨ Results & benefits
Can bin cleaning help with odors?

Yes. Washing and deodorizing target the residue that causes persistent smells, although new household waste will naturally introduce odors again over time.

Does bin cleaning deter flies, bugs and rodents?

Removing food residue and organic buildup makes bins less attractive to many insects and pests. Cleaning helps reduce attractants, but it cannot guarantee pests will never visit a bin.

How often should residential bins be cleaned?

The right schedule depends on household size, cart type, weather, contents and the amount of odor or residue. Monthly or quarterly care may suit higher-use homes, while others may prefer a one-time reset. Final plan options will be explained before launch.

Does professional cleaning remove every germ?

No responsible service should promise complete germ removal without a validated product and process. Our published claims will match the actual cleaning and deodorizing products used when service launches.
