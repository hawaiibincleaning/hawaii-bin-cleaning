Why equipment matters

Not a pressure washer in the back of a pickup.

Professional bin cleaning is a complete process: lift the cart, reach the interior, supply the water and manage the dirty runoff.

Our truck was configured specifically for residential bin-cleaning routes. The lifter and cleaning heads work over a contained aluminum hopper, fresh water travels with the truck and used wash water is stored separately for responsible handling.

🚛

2011 Ford E-450 Super Duty

Commercial chassis with a 12-foot stainless-steel bed and a purpose-built residential bin-cleaning system.
