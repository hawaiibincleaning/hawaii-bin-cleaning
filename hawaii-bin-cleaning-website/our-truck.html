Why equipment matters

Not a pressure washer in the back of a pickup.

A professional bin-cleaning route has to do more than spray water.

The system must position the cart, reach interior surfaces, carry a dependable water supply, manage the runoff and repeat the process efficiently from stop to stop. Our lifter and cleaning heads work over a contained aluminum hopper, fresh water travels with the truck and used wash water is stored separately.

Onboard wastewater recovery

The dirty water should leave with us.

Cleaning a grimy bin creates wash water carrying the residue that was inside it.

Our equipment is designed to recover that water onboard rather than leave it flowing across the driveway or toward a storm drain. Final disposal procedures will follow the authorizations and requirements that apply to our operation.

Separate onboard tanks support the cleaning route and keep recovered gray water contained for responsible handling.
