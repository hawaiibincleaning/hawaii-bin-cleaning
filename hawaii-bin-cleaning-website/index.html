Not a hose-down. A purpose-built system.

The difference is built into the truck.

Hawaii Bin Cleaning is investing in a fully equipped Ford E-450 residential bin-cleaning truck—not a pressure washer placed in the back of a pickup.

The system combines specialized Bolondi cleaning heads, a dual hydraulic bin lifter, commercial washing power and separate tanks for fresh and recovered water. That means a more consistent curbside process without asking you to provide a hose or a place for filthy runoff to go.
