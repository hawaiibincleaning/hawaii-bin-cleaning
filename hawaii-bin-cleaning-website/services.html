Our curbside process

Three simple steps for the customer. Serious equipment behind them.

Professional bin cleaning works best after collection, at the curb, with equipment designed to lift the cart and take the dirty water away. Our truck is built to do exactly that.

Read common service questions →
  1. 1

    Book and leave carts out

    After your normal collection, keep empty carts accessible at the curb for the scheduled route.

  2. 2

    We lift and clean

    The dual hydraulic lifter positions carts over specialized Bolondi heads powered by an 8 GPM, 4,000 PSI cleaning system.

  3. 3

    We deodorize and recover runoff

    Cleaned carts return curbside while dirty wash water is captured in a 525-gallon gray-water tank.

Why the truck matters

Built for bins from tank to cleaning head.

A consumer pressure washer depends on the operator's angle, the property's water supply and somewhere for runoff to go. Our Ford E-450 carries 550 gallons of fresh water, recovers up to 525 gallons of gray water and uses equipment engineered for residential carts.
